Financial Mastery Callouts

Pay Yourself First

Instead of saving "whatever is left over," set up an automatic transfer of your target savings percentage (e.g., 15%) on payday before spending on anything else.

Why Income Growth + Investing = Wealth

Increasing your salary gives you more fuel (money to invest), but compounding gives you the engine. Combined over 40–50 years, you build multi-million dollar security.
