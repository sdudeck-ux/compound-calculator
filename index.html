The Power of "Ramping Up"

You don't need a huge salary right now to build wealth. Saving $50–$100 in high school builds the discipline, and increasing contributions as your career grows creates compound momentum!

Roth IRA for Young Earners

If you earn money from a part-time job, you can open a Roth IRA. Contributions grow 100% tax-free, making it the most powerful tool for young investors.
