Key Financial Concepts

FDIC Insurance

Savings Accounts, HYSAs, and CDs are backed by the federal government up to $250,000 per depositor. You cannot lose your principal money in these accounts.

Liquidity

Refers to how quickly you can access your cash. Savings accounts are highly liquid. CDs lock your cash away for a set term. Stocks are liquid, but selling during a crash can cause losses.

No-Load Index Funds & Expense Ratios

Index funds (like VOO) buy a piece of the 500 biggest U.S. companies. "No-load" means zero sales fee. Look for ultra-low expense ratios (e.g., 0.03%) so fees don't eat your profits.

Roth IRA for Working Teens

If you have a job (even part-time), you can open a Roth IRA. The money you invest grows 100% tax-free, and withdrawals in retirement are completely untaxed!
